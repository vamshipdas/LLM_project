ABSTRACT 
Modern organizations face increasing cybersecurity threats such as phishing, ransomware, insider attacks, and 
unauthorized access attempts. Security teams often rely on Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to respond to 
incidents consistently and in compliance with organizational policies. However, during high-pressure situations, 
analysts may struggle to quickly identify the correct procedures, leading to delayed responses and human error. 
This project proposes an AI-assisted cybersecurity incident response framework that combines Retrieval-
Augmented Generation (RAG) with Large Language Models (LLMs) to provide SOP-aware incident handling 
guidance. Instead of relying on fully autonomous response execution, the system acts as an intelligent decision-
support assistant for security analysts. The framework retrieves relevant SOP sections from a centralized SOP 
repository and uses an LLM to generate contextual recommendations, incident summaries, severity analysis, 
and step-by-step response guidance. 
The proposed system focuses on explainability, auditability, and human-in-the-loop security operations. Every 
recommendation generated by the LLM is accompanied by references to the corresponding SOP sections used 
during retrieval. A confidence-scoring mechanism helps analysts identify uncertain recommendations that 
require additional review. The framework also maintains detailed audit logs containing alert information, 
retrieved SOP references, analyst actions, and timestamps for compliance and forensic purposes. 
The system uses simulated cybersecurity alerts and attack scenarios to demonstrate incident triage and response 
workflows. By integrating RAG-based knowledge retrieval with SOP-guided reasoning, the project aims to 
reduce response time, improve consistency in incident handling, and minimize human error while ensuring that 
analysts remain in control of all critical decisions.

INTRODUCTION 
Cybersecurity incidents are becoming increasingly sophisticated and frequent in modern enterprise environments. 
Organizations depend on Security Operations Centers (SOCs) and incident response teams to detect, analyze, and 
mitigate threats before they cause significant damage. To ensure consistency and compliance, organizations define 
Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) that guide analysts during incident handling. 
Traditional incident response processes are largely manual and depend heavily on analyst experience and 
decision-making under pressure. Existing automated solutions often rely on static rule -based workflows that 
struggle to adapt to new or evolving threats. At the sam e time, fully autonomous AI -driven response systems 
introduce security and reliability concerns due to hallucinations, incorrect recommendations, and lack of 
transparency. 
To address these challenges, this project proposes an SOP -guided cybersecurity assis tant using Retrieval -
Augmented Generation (RAG) and Large Language Models (LLMs). The system retrieves relevant SOP content 
based on incoming incident alerts and uses the LLM to generate contextual, explainable recommendations for 
analysts. 
The proposed framework emphasizes: 
 Human-in-the-loop decision making  
 SOP-grounded AI reasoning  
 Explainable recommendations  
 Auditability and traceability  
 Secure handling of incident data  
Rather than replacing human analysts, the system acts as an intelligent assistan t that improves response 
consistency and reduces incident handling delays.

PROBLEM STATEMENT 
Enterprise incident response processes often suffer from delayed decision -making, inconsistent handling 
procedures, and dependence on individual analyst expertise. During active security incidents, analysts must 
quickly identify appropriate SOPs, assess incident severity, and determine suitable response actions. This process 
can become time-consuming and error-prone, especially when dealing with large volumes of alerts. 
Existing automated response systems typically rely on predefined playbooks and rigid workflows that cannot 
easily adapt to evolving attack patterns. On the other hand, fully autonomous AI systems may produce unreliable 
or unsafe recommendations due to hallucinations or lack of contextual grounding. 
Furthermore, many existing systems lack: 
 SOP-aware contextual guidance  
 Explainable AI-generated recommendations  
 Traceable audit logs linked to procedures  
 Human validation mechanisms  
 Efficient retrieval of relevant security procedures  
Therefore, there is a need for a secure and explainable AI -assisted framework that uses Retrieval -Augmented 
Generation (RAG) to retrieve relevant SOP knowledge and assist analysts during cybersecurity incident response.

PROPOSED METHODOLOGY 
Step 1: SOP Collection and Processing 
 Collect cybersecurity SOP documents in PDF, DOCX, or TXT format.  
 Extract and preprocess text content from SOPs.  
 Split SOP content into smaller searchable chunks.  
Step 2: Vector Embedding and Storage 
 Convert SOP chunks into vector embeddings using embedding models.  
 Store embeddings in a vector database for efficient retrieval.  
Step 3: Alert Simulation 
 Generate simulated cybersecurity alerts such as:  
o phishing attacks  
o malware detection  
o brute-force attempts  
o unauthorized access  
Step 4: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) 
 User submits an incident alert.  
 System retrieves relevant SOP sections from the vector database.  
 Retrieved SOP context is provided to the LLM.  
Step 5: LLM-Based Recommendation 
The LLM generates: 
 Incident summary  
 Severity level  
 Recommended actions  
 SOP references  
 Analyst guidance

SUMMARY 
This project presents an SOP-guided cybersecurity incident response assistant using 
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and Large Language Models (LLMs). The 
framework focuses on assisting security analysts by retrieving relevant SOP knowledge and 
generating explainable recommendations for incident handling. 
Unlike fully autonomous systems, the proposed framework follows a human-in-the-loop 
approach where analysts remain responsible for validating all critical actions. By combining 
RAG-based retrieval, LLM reasoning, explainable recommendations, and audit logging, the 
system improves response consistency, reduces delays, and strengthens operational security 
practices. 
The project demonstrates how AI can be safely integrated into cybersecurity workflows while 
maintaining transparency, procedural compliance, and analyst control.
